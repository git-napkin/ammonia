Ammonia

Runtime tweak loader for macOS Apple Silicon. Hooks posix_spawn inside launchd and loads enabled .dylib tweaks into UI apps, including sandboxed ones.

Includes

ammoniaInjector (one-shot LaunchDaemon)
libinject.dylibposix_spawn hook in launchd
libopener.dylibPer-process tweak loader
Ammonia.appGUI in /Applications
tweaks//private/var/ammonia/core/tweaks
